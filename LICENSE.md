LoNSeMonSy is licensed **AGPL-3.0-or-later**.

This is not a free choice: the project builds directly on Wazuh (GPL-2.0) and Grafana
(AGPL-3.0). Distributing an appliance that bundles Grafana means the combined work is
governed by the AGPL's network-copyleft terms. Choosing AGPL-3.0 for LoNSeMonSy keeps
us compatible and honest about it.

Business implication (see `docs/OVERVIEW.md`): revenue comes from **prebuilt hardware
and support**, not from closing the source — the same model Security Onion Solutions
uses. The AGPL does not prevent selling appliances; it prevents making the software
proprietary.
